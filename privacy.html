1. Overview & Commitment to Financial Privacy

At VeraLedger Advisory LLP ("VeraLedger", "we", "us"), protecting the confidentiality of your statutory identity, taxation records, and commercial books is central to everything we do.

This policy describes our data processing practices under the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023, Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices) Rules, 2011, and the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI) professional ethical standards.

2. Categories of Information We Collect

To fulfill statutory filing requirements with the Income Tax Department and GST Network (GSTN), we collect only necessary financial records:

  • Identity & Contact: Full legal name, date of birth, PAN, Aadhaar linking verification status, email address, and mobile number.
  • Financial Records: Form 16 / 16A, Annual Information Statement (AIS/TIS), broker capital gains statements, bank statements showing interest, and foreign asset disclosures.
  • Business Data: GSTIN outward sales invoices, purchase ledgers, trial balances, and Director Identification Numbers (DIN).
  • Billing Information: Transaction identifiers, invoice history, and settlement confirmations (card numbers and UPI MPINs are handled directly by PCI-DSS Level 1 certified payment gateways and are never stored on our servers).

3. How We Process Your Financial Records

Your data is processed strictly for the following professional purposes:

  • Modeling tax calculations and dual-regime optimization under Section 115BAC to ensure you pay the legal minimum tax.
  • Compiling and validating ITR forms (ITR-1 through ITR-4) and GST returns (GSTR-1, GSTR-3B).
  • Submitting authenticated return XML/JSON schema files to the Central Processing Centre (CPC) and GST portal on your behalf.
  • Generating official filing acknowledgement receipts and providing audit shield notice assistance.

4. Vault Encryption & Security Safeguards

Bank-Grade 256-Bit SSL Protection: All web sessions and file transfers utilize TLS 1.3 encryption with 256-bit AES cryptographic ciphers. Uploaded files are segregated into encrypted cloud storage buckets accessible solely by the assigned senior CPA.

We enforce multi-factor authentication (MFA) across our practice team, conduct quarterly third-party vulnerability assessments, and maintain continuous access logs for all document downloads.

5. Strict No-Sale & No-Brokering Guarantee

Unlike commercial aggregators, VeraLedger does not sell, rent, monetize, or disclose your financial records or personal information to third-party lenders, personal loan brokers, insurance agents, or credit card telemarketers.

Data is transmitted exclusively to statutory bodies (CBDT e-filing portal, GSTN system, and Ministry of Corporate Affairs) as authorized by your explicit filing submission.

6. Document Retention & Permanent Deletion

Under Rule 114E of the Income Tax Rules and ICAI practice directives, working papers and statutory filing receipts are retained for a minimum of seven (7) Assessment Years to defend clients against potential scrutiny or assessment reassessments.

Upon expiration of statutory retention requirements, working drafts and redundant scans are permanently shredded using cryptographic zero-fill procedures.

7. Your Statutory Rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, you possess the right to:

  • Request an extract of all personal data held within your VeraLedger workspace.
  • Rectify inaccurate personal information or outdated bank details.
  • Revoke consent for optional notifications and promotional tax updates.
