Pricing FAQs

Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know about fees, payment methods, refund policies, and additional scope.

Are there any hidden costs or surprise fees after I submit my application?

No. VeraLedger operates on strict all-inclusive pricing. The fee displayed when you select your service covers document parsing, comparative dual-regime tax computation, CPA review, electronic submission, and full receipt archiving. If an unexpected statutory complexity arises (such as undisclosed foreign assets), we discuss and approve any scope adjustment with you in advance.

What is your refund policy if I cancel before my return is filed?

We offer a 100% money-back guarantee if you cancel before our chartered accountants begin preparing your formal tax draft. If draft preparation has already commenced, a nominal 25% administrative fee applies, and the remaining 75% is refunded within 3–5 business days to your original payment method.

Do you charge extra if I switched employers and have multiple Form 16s?

Our Professional Tier (₹2,499) completely covers multiple employers, switch salary overlaps, and bonus/severance reconciliations at no additional surcharge. You do not need to purchase multiple return filings.

What payment methods do you accept?

We support all leading Indian digital payment methods including UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM), all major Credit/Debit Cards (Visa, MasterCard, RuPay, Amex), Net Banking across 50+ banks, and corporate NEFT/RTGS bank transfers with instant GST invoices.

How do I know my payment is secure?

All transactions are processed through RBI-authorized payment aggregators conforming to PCI-DSS Level 1 compliance and 256-bit bank-grade SSL encryption. VeraLedger never stores your raw card numbers or UPI PINs.
