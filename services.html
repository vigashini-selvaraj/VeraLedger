Common Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Clear answers to common questions about our service scope, document onboarding, security, and timelines.

What documents do I need to prepare before starting my tax filing?

For salaried individuals, your Form 16 (Part A & B) and bank statements are the main requirements. If you have capital gains, investment statements from your broker or crypto exchanges are needed. For businesses and freelancers, your bank statements, expense invoices, and GSTR summaries are required. Our team can also fetch your AIS and Form 26AS directly with your authorization.

How do you determine whether the New or Old tax regime is better for me?

We run a side-by-side mathematical calculation comparing your gross income against both regime slabs. If your total eligible deductions under Section 80C, 80D, 24(b) home loan interest, and HRA exceed the threshold where the lower slabs of the New Regime win out, we recommend the Old Regime. You receive a complete comparison summary before filing.

What happens if I receive an Income Tax or GST scrutiny notice?

Every return filed through VeraLedger includes our comprehensive notice protection. If the tax department sends an inquiry or intimation, our chartered accountants review the notice, draft a formal legal response, and represent you before the tax authorities at no surprise extra charge.

How is my confidential financial information protected?

We implement bank-grade 256-bit AES encryption across all document vaults and data transfers. Our infrastructure conforms to strict ICAI confidentiality protocols, and we enforce a strict non-disclosure guarantee with zero third-party data sharing.

What is your standard turnaround time for completing a return?

Most individual ITR filings and monthly GST reconciliations are prepared and reviewed within 36 to 48 hours once all required documents are uploaded. For complex corporate books or multi-year audit rectifications, we provide a structured milestone timeline during our initial consultation.

Can I transition to VeraLedger from my current accountant in the middle of the financial year?

Yes. Mid-year onboarding is seamless. We coordinate the intake of your opening trial balances, previous year tax returns, and current year GST invoices without interrupting your recurring operations or missing filing deadlines.
