Common Enquiries

Frequently asked questions.

Answers to typical questions about booking consultations, office visits, and document intake.

Can I schedule a 1-on-1 consultation virtually rather than visiting in person?

Yes! Over 85% of our client consultations take place via secure video conference (Google Meet or Zoom). You receive a calendar invite with screen-sharing capabilities so we can review your Form 16, AIS, or GST registers together in real time.

What documents should I prepare before contacting or calling?

You do not need everything to begin. For individual tax filing, having your PAN and Form 16/salary slips is helpful. For GST and bookkeeping queries, having your GSTIN and latest monthly turnover estimate will allow our CPAs to provide precise recommendations on the first call.

How quickly will I receive a response after submitting this form?

During standard business hours (Mon–Fri, 9:30 AM–6:30 PM), our team responds within 2 hours. Messages submitted after hours or over the weekend are prioritized first thing on the following business morning.

Can I visit the Chennai office for in-person document handover?

Yes, we welcome in-person visits at our Guindy headquarters. We recommend booking a slot in advance using the contact form or phone line so your designated Chartered Accountant is pre-briefed and available when you arrive.

Is my enquiry and financial data kept strictly confidential?

Yes. All member firms and practitioners at VeraLedger are bound by the statutory Code of Ethics of the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI). We never disclose client details or documents to any third parties.
