What you will learn in this guide:

  • How to retrieve and reconcile your Form 16 against AIS/TIS without discrepancy notices.
  • Which deduction certificates are strictly required vs. optional under statutory scrutiny.
  • Pre-submission validation steps to expedite refund processing within 10 to 14 days.

Preparing for tax filing is fundamentally about having the right numbers confirmed before you open any filing software. When your paperwork is gathered in advance, filing transforms from an anxious scramble into a calm, methodical 15-minute verification.

1. Primary income records

Your gross receipts and salary certificates form the baseline of your statutory return. Depending on whether you are salaried, freelancing, or managing a registered enterprise, collect these essential records:

01

Form 16 (Part A & Part B)

Issued by your employer, detailing gross salary, standard deduction, professional tax, and exact TDS withheld under your PAN.

02

Annual Information Statement (AIS) & TIS

Directly synchronized from the income tax e-filing portal to capture high-value savings interest, dividends, and securities transactions.

03

Form 26AS Tax Credit Ledger

Your consolidated tax passbook proving all tax deducted at source by employers, banks, and clients has been remitted to the government.

"The single biggest cause of Section 143(1) tax adjustment notices is omitted bank savings account interest that was already reported to the department via AIS." — Priya Sharma, FCA

2. Deductions and tax-saving proofs

If you are evaluating the Old Tax Regime to see if deductions beat the lower rates of the New Regime under Section 115BAC, assemble your receipts early:

  • Section 80C Receipts: PPF deposits, EPF contributions, ELSS mutual fund statements, life insurance premiums, and principal repayment statements for home loans.
  • Section 80D Health Insurance: Premium receipts for self, spouse, dependent children, and parents (with senior citizen add-on benefits up to ₹50,000).
  • House Rent Allowance (HRA): Rent agreements and rent payment receipts, alongside the landlord's PAN if annual rent exceeds ₹1,00,000.
  • Home Loan Interest (Section 24b): Provisional interest certificate issued by your lender for owner-occupied property up to ₹2,00,000.

3. Final pre-submission checkpoint

Before submitting the return or transmitting it to the Centralized Processing Center (CPC), run a 3-point accuracy check:

First, confirm that interest income from all active savings accounts and fixed deposits is declared under "Income from Other Sources". Second, check that exempt allowances match your salary slip specifications. Finally, ensure your Aadhaar-PAN linkage is operative so e-verification via Aadhaar OTP completes seamlessly.

Key Takeaways & Action Items

  • Pre-fill Verification: Always reconcile your Form 16 against both AIS (Annual Information Statement) and Form 26AS before filing.
  • Dual Regime Math: Keep documentation for Section 80C, 80D, and HRA handy even if you suspect the New Tax Regime is more beneficial.
  • Bank Account Validation: Ensure your refund bank account is pre-validated on the Income Tax Portal with matched PAN names.
Expert Advisory Note · Section 87A Rebate

Under the revised New Tax Regime (Section 115BAC), individuals with taxable income up to ₹7,00,000 pay zero tax after Section 87A rebate. Together with the enhanced standard deduction of ₹75,000, salaried filers earning up to ₹7,75,000 incur zero statutory tax liability.
