Which documents are mandatory for salaried ITR?

You will need Form 16 (Part A & B) from your employer, your PAN card, bank statement showing savings account interest, and annual AIS/TIS downloaded from the Income Tax portal.

How fast will a CPA review my uploaded records?

Standard applications are reviewed by your assigned senior CPA within 24 to 48 hours. If any deduction receipts or Form 26AS mismatch items appear, you'll receive a direct query in your workspace.
